# Xtrim

**Activity tracking with a community around it**

Xtrim brings personal fitness activity and shared progress into one app. People can record activities, follow their history, and take part in clubs, events, and challenges.

## Product experiences

- Record activities with time, distance, pace, route, and progress details where supported by the activity and device.
- Review activity history, goals, and personal progress.
- Find clubs, events, challenges, and other community activity.
- Share selected activity and take part in group goals.
- Manage profiles and community participation in the app.
- Support public product pages and administration alongside the mobile experience.

Xtrim's product direction combines individual training tools with social motivation, so members can take part on their own or alongside a group.

## Engineering scope

The mobile app is built with Flutter and Dart. Supabase and PostgreSQL support application data and backend workflows, with separate web experiences for public and administrative needs.

## Technology

Flutter and Dart, Supabase, PostgreSQL, and web tooling.
