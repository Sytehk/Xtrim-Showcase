# Xtrim

**Fitness tracking with a community to keep moving**

Xtrim brings personal activity tracking together with clubs, events, challenges, and shared progress. It is designed for people who want to record their own training and find a community around running, riding, walking, and other activities.

## Train on your own or together

The activity experience supports recording a session and reviewing its history. Depending on the activity and device, a recording can include elapsed time, distance, pace, route, elevation, goals, splits, and pause controls. Members can revisit previous activity, track progress, and share selected results.

The community experience gives members ways to find clubs, join group challenges, discover events, and follow activity from people they care about. Clubs can organize around location or sport, while challenges add a shared goal members can work toward at their own pace.

## Product surfaces

- **Home** brings progress, activity, and community updates into one place.
- **Start and activity history** support recording and reviewing workouts.
- **Explore** helps members find clubs, events, challenges, and people.
- **Routes and goals** support planning and progress during an activity.
- **Calendar and challenge views** help members keep track of upcoming group activity and personal targets.
- **Public web and administration tools** support product information and community operations.

The recording flow has to deal with real device conditions, not just a static workout form. Location permissions, GPS quality, battery use, background recording, manual and automatic pauses, and interruptions can all affect what the member sees and what gets saved. Xtrim's product and engineering work treats those states as part of the activity experience.

## Engineering

The mobile app is built with Flutter and Dart. Supabase and PostgreSQL support user, activity, and community data, with supporting web experiences for public and administrative workflows. The product also includes health and activity-data integration work and community operations for moderation and official content.

## Technology

Flutter and Dart, Supabase, PostgreSQL, location and activity services, and web tooling.
